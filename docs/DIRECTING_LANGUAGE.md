# NOVA Directing Language

Camera framing includes establishing, wide, medium, close-up, extreme close-up,
over-the-shoulder, profile, insert, and point-of-view shots. Angles are low, high,
or eye-level. Movement includes static, pan, tilt, dolly, tracking, push-in,
pull-out, handheld, crane, and aerial.

Performance plans describe emotional objective, visible behavior, body language,
eye-line, gesture, tempo, restraint, delivery, reaction, and subtext. Pacing plans
describe scene rhythm, target shot duration, escalation, pauses, reveal timing,
action density, emotional intensity, and transition rhythm.

Continuity state carries identity, wardrobe, props, environment, lighting,
weather, time of day, injuries, emotion, spatial position, dialogue state, and
previous-shot details. These terms describe intent and never select a renderer.
